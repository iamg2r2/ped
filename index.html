IQAC Pedagogical & Assessment Evaluation Rubric Matrix

How every submitted report is systematically scored out of 100 points

1. Pedagogical Innovation & Active Learning 25 Pts

Shift from teacher-centric to student-centric learning: Flipped classroom, project-based learning, inquiry, collaborative problem solving.

2. Bloom's Taxonomy Cognitive Demand 25 Pts

Proportion of assessment targeting Higher-Order Thinking Skills (HOTS: Analyze, Evaluate, Create) vs Lower-Order (Remember, Understand).

3. Outcome-Based Education (OBE) & CO-PO Rigor 20 Pts

Alignment with Course Outcomes (COs) and Program Outcomes (POs), transparent rubrics for grading, attainment threshold tracking.

4. ICT Tools & Digital Learning Integration 15 Pts

Effective deployment of LMS (Moodle/Canvas), interactive coding pods, virtual labs, simulation tools, or digital peer reviews.

5. Student Feedback & Remedial Loop 15 Pts

Student satisfaction rating, evidence of continuous feedback, dedicated remedial actions for slow learners, and enrichment for fast learners.
